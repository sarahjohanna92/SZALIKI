Meeting · everyday life

Life goes on

Close to the front, everyday life and danger exist side by side. You can hear gunfire, there are anti-drone nets hanging over the roads, and there are signs of further attacks on the buildings. And yet the shops open in the morning, people go to work, shop and invite us for tea. We bring help, but what is equally important is that we can sit, listen and be with them for a while. Life goes on - although no one knows what the next day will bring.

Help · animals

Help doesn't end with the car

We met Mrs. Sveta during our trips to Kramatorsk. Despite the war, she took care of dogs that needed food and care. We came back to her with food, also thanks to cooperation with K9 Rescue Ukraine. For us, help doesn't end when the car leaves - we remember about the people and animals we meet. The dogs and their owner are now in a safer place. We were grateful for the trust they placed in us.

Children · community

Small things, great joy

In a place that is formally a school, children can find much more than lessons: also therapy, help and development activities. The center was moved from the Kupyansk area to Kharkov when the front line approached the former location. Kharkov itself is also under fire. Despite this, every day someone opens the door and creates a space for children where they can learn, play and return to their usual rhythm, at least for a moment. Toys, candy and notebooks are little things - but these are the moments that childhood is made of.

Team · logistics

Help starts with listening

Before we set off, we talk to people who know the needs on site. We ask what is really missing, collect things and plan a common path. The situation may change, and safety must be assessed on an ongoing basis - sometimes we go further, sometimes we change plans. We do not provide ready-made answers. We listen first, because help makes sense when it responds to what people actually need.

Cooperation · medicine

Together we can deliver more

This path never belongs to one person or one organization. The Team Kraków Foundation collects, sorts and prepares material aid, and we pack it into the car and deliver it further. We are also supported by people and companies who help us get on the road - sometimes by donating necessary things, sometimes by renting a bus. Everyone contributes their part. Thanks to this, help can reach more people, and trust is created between teams, which remains for a long time.

Everyday life · place

A place that used to be teeming with life

We look at broken windows, torn walls and rubble, but we try to remember what came before them. In these apartments, someone was coming back after work, feeding a child, stacking blocks or getting ready for a date. Someone was doing homework at the table, someone was waiting for a loved one. These weren't just buildings - these were people's homes and ordinary days. Today, sometimes only rubble remains.
