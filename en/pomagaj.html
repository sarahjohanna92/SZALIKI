Every contribution

We are the NONPROFIT SZALIKI Association. We work from the ground up and close to people: organizing humanitarian aid and delivering it in person where it is needed. Your support helps cover travel costs and meet needs we learn about on the ground.

Best option: a direct bank transfer

A transfer to the association’s account lets us put more of your donation toward our work. Zrzutka charges a fee, so if you can, please choose a direct transfer. Every contribution becomes practical help: fuel, essential supplies, and items delivered to people.
